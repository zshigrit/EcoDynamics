I earned a **Ph.D. in Ecology and Global Change** from the University of Oklahoma in 2015. I later held postdoctoral research appointments at **Oak Ridge National Laboratory** and the **University of California, Irvine**. I am now a Research Scientist at the **Institute for Environmental Genomics, University of Oklahoma**.

My research brings together ecosystem ecology, soil biogeochemistry, quantitative analysis, and terrestrial carbon-cycle modeling. I work with field measurements, radiocarbon data, and Earth system models to study how environmental change affects carbon storage and ecosystem function.

See [Research](research.md) for current themes and [Publications](publications.md) for selected papers.
