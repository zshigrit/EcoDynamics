My research connects ecological observations with models to understand terrestrial carbon dynamics under global change.

## Soil carbon and radiocarbon

Radiocarbon measurements help reveal the age and turnover of carbon stored at different soil depths. I use these constraints to examine the processes behind soil carbon storage and to evaluate model predictions.

## Global change and ecosystem responses

Warming, altered precipitation, nutrient availability, and vegetation management can interact to shape plant production and soil carbon dynamics. Field experiments provide a way to test those interactions and identify mechanisms that are difficult to infer from large-scale patterns alone.

## Ecosystem and Earth system models

I work with process-based models and model–data comparisons to assess carbon-cycle predictions. An important part of this work is asking whether models that produce similar global totals also reproduce the regional patterns and underlying processes observed in nature.
