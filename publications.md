## Selected papers

- **Shi, Z., et al. (2024).** [Global-Scale Convergence Obscures Inconsistencies in Soil Carbon Change Predicted by Earth System Models](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023AV001068). *AGU Advances.* Compares soil-carbon predictions across Earth system models and examines disagreement hidden by global averages.
- **Shi, Z., et al. (2020).** [The age distribution of global soil carbon inferred from radiocarbon measurements](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-020-0596-z). *Nature Geoscience.* Uses radiocarbon observations to constrain the age of soil carbon worldwide.
- **Shi, Z., et al. (2016).** [Dual mechanisms regulate ecosystem stability under decade-long warming and hay harvest](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms11973). *Nature Communications.* Examines how warming and hay harvest influence the stability of grassland production.

This is a selection of publications, not a complete bibliography.
